# Architecture

Pinch is a Next.js 16 app (App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, TypeScript, pnpm), deployed on Vercel. One tool page and a terms page, route handlers under `/api/pin/`, no database but a private Vercel Blob store for JSON documents and, for signed-in buyers, their collections' zips. It began as the `/pin` route of the GenUI repository and was moved out whole on 2026-10-19; nothing of that site remains here.

## Repo map

- `src/app/layout.tsx`: fonts (Geist, Geist Mono through `next/font`), the page metadata (title "Pinch: tag screenshots for your agent", description, Open Graph, icon `/pin-notes/icon.svg`) and the theme provider. `src/app/page.tsx` renders the app at `/`. `src/app/robots.ts` allows `/` and disallows `/api/`. `src/app/global.css` holds the tokens (light and dark, `@custom-variant dark`), the Base UI state variants, the radius scale and the base layer.
- `src/components/site/theme-provider.tsx`: next-themes (`attribute="class"`, dark by default, system honoured, storage key `genui-theme`, kept so existing browsers keep their choice) and the `D` hotkey that toggles the theme outside fields. `theme-toggle.tsx` is the header button.
- `src/components/ui/`: `button.tsx`, `switch.tsx`, `tooltip.tsx` on Base UI, in the shadcn style (the only three Pinch uses).
- `src/components/pin/`: the app. `pin-app.tsx` (the workspace: header, landing, filmstrip, stage, notes panel, toast, shortcuts dialog, history with undo and redo, the Pin / Area / Arrow toolbar, the privacy, shortcuts and new-batch dialogs, the desktop-only screen on phones, the 100-image cap), `dialog.tsx` (the focus-trapped dialog and the shared 44px hit-area class), `collections.tsx` (Your rules, the person's one collection, shown in the images' place: adding the open notes, reading them into rules, the rules by topic with their evidence and the pencil, slicing, the preview's placeholders, the export dialog, moving this browser's rules into the account) and `collections-store.ts` (its own IndexedDB database `pinch-collections`: one record per collection, each batch's export zip by its id), `topic-style.ts` (the topic tags' tints and hit area), `topic-picker.tsx` (the tags a note or a rule is about), `account.tsx` (`/api/pin/me` in the page, the landing's sign-in and buy gate, the Account dialog), `cloud-store.ts` and `collections-backend.ts` (the account's collections through the cloud routes, and the interface both stores share), `drop-folder.tsx` (the landing's folder animation), `brand.tsx` (the wordmark), `store.ts` (the session in IndexedDB: database `pin-notes`, images as Blobs by id, the assets and marks as one record written on every change) and `render.ts` (canvas work: image preparation, the marked copy of each image, a crop per note, the crops sent for suggestions, the export zip and download).
- `src/lib/pin/`: pure code, tested in `__tests__/`. `model.ts` (assets, marks as dots, areas or arrows, sources, topics (`TOPICS`), suggestions, `settleCrop`, the export's README, `AGENTS.md` and `notes.json` text), `sheet.ts` (contact sheet row planning), `zip.ts` (a stored-entry zip writer), `measure.ts` (Measure spacing: the element at a note and the gaps around it, the screenshot scale), `rhythm.ts` (Check spacing between items: the repeated items in an area and the gaps between them), `suggest.ts` (the prompt, its Zod output schema with the topics, and the filter that drops sentences about the overlay), `unzip.ts` (reads a zip back: stored entries, or deflated ones through `DecompressionStream`), `import.ts` (an export's `notes.json` back into assets and marks, and into a collection's notes), `learnings.ts` (the taste library: batches of notes, rules with the notes they draw on, a reading applied, merging, slicing by topic and tag, the rules file `design-rules.md` and `rules.json`), `learn.ts` (the reading's request, prompt, schema, tidying and preview), `foundation.ts` (the expert design foundation the reading uses, D90: principles, each with where it applies, where it gives way, the evidence that justifies it and its sources, and the shorthand that points at them; kept apart from the collection, which stores only principle ids), `visitor.ts` (the trusted visitor address), `notes-log.ts` (what the browser sends and how a day's document takes it), `notes-store.ts` (the day documents in Blob), `notes-tags.ts` (areas, Jev questions, tags, month summary) and `notes-tagger.ts` (the calls to Jev after a save), `ai-budget.ts` (one daily ledger per AI step).
- `src/lib/`: `blob-doc.ts` (JSON documents in the private Blob store changed by compare-and-swap on the ETag, in memory without Blob credentials), `budget.ts` (prices per model, `costUsd`, `refusal`, `visitorKey` salted by `VISITOR_SALT`), `jev.ts` (`askJevQuestions`, `THRESHOLD`, `JEV_MODEL`, `JEV_ENDPOINT`), `auth.ts` (Basic auth against `ADMIN_PASSWORD`, realm "Pinch admin"; `isSameOriginWrite`), `image.ts` (`sniffImage`), `utils.ts` (`cn`). Tests in `src/lib/__tests__/`.
- `public/pin-notes/`: `icon.svg` (favicon, the wordmark's "p") and `landing.webp` (the dusk painting behind the landing card, shown in dark mode) and `landing-night.webp` (the night version, faded over it in light mode).
- `scripts/`: `jev-stub.mjs` (a local stand-in for Jev) and `format.mjs` (Prettier on named files).
- `docs/`: this file, [decisions.md](decisions.md), [design.md](design.md), [status.md](status.md).

## How it works

Client side: the session (images as Blobs, assets and marks as one record) lives in IndexedDB and is written on every change; images removed from the session stay until no undo snapshot holds them (`pinch-orphans` in `localStorage` names the ones waiting). The export draws a marked copy of each image, a crop per note (its edges settled on quiet bands of the image, `settleCrop`) and a contact sheet of the batch on canvas and zips them with `README.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `notes.json`. On a dot the number sits beside the point on a leader; on an area just outside its corner; an arrow runs from its numbered tail to its head. An area note may also carry a spacing check between its items (`rhythm.ts`, on the same grid), drawn and exported the same way. A note may carry measured spacing (`measure.ts`, on a one-pixel luma grid sampled when the author asks; drawn on its crop and on the stage, written into the README and `notes.json` with the image's scale). Each image may carry a source (Ours, Competitor, Other product, Concept; "made by" and a link stay in the browser). Each note may carry topics (Visual, Layout, Copy, Interaction, Flow, Accessibility), picked as chips or taken with a suggestion; they go into the export, never to the server. A Pinch export (`.zip`) dropped or chosen opens into the session: its images and notes join what is there (`unzip.ts`, `import.ts`), which is how a batch moves to another browser. Your rules (D79, D86, D87, D88), one collection per person, take the images' place from the header's Your rules button, the image screen's main one. Opening it keeps the open images' new notes (by image and words, so a note is never kept twice) as an export zip, behind the scenes, for the close-ups. Every note not yet read goes to `/api/pin/learn` thirty at a time with the rules so far, on its own; each reading strengthens rules (their new notes counted as `fresh` until the evidence is opened), adds rules, or adds one flagged as a conflict with the rule it pulls against. A rule holds its notes as references (`batchId/ref`), many to many; it is guidance as soon as it is written, and the pencil edits its words, where it holds, its topics and the author's own tags (D88). Rules show by first topic and slice by one topic or tag. Before buying, the hidden rules show as placeholders; bought, they go and their notes are read again in full. The export dialog picks topics and tags and zips `design-rules.md`, `rules.json` and each rule's close-ups, read from their zips, as `evidence/r01-1.jpg` and on, or copies the rules as text for a chat. Several collections kept from before D86 are merged into the oldest when the view opens, and learnings from before D87 become rules. Bought and signed in, the rules live in the account (D81), and this browser's move in on their own. The images' keys are off while the rules show. Keys: arrows or J/K move between images, 1–9 open a note, N adds one, A toggles the Arrow tool, S cycles the source, Delete removes the open note, Escape closes it, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo, ? lists them.

## Routes

- `GET /` the tool. `GET /robots.txt`.
- `POST /api/pin/suggest` (multipart `image`, `context`, `label?`, `source?`, `shape?`): drafts what works and what doesn't, and one or two topics, for an empty note from a close-up and the whole screen (1024px and 768px JPEGs, marked in magenta), on `PIN_SUGGEST_MODEL` (default `claude-haiku-5-5`, no fallback: a refusal just means no suggestion). Stores nothing. Off with `PIN_SUGGEST=0`, `PIN_AI=0` or no key. Ledger `pin-notes/suggest/<day>.json`: `PIN_SUGGEST_DAILY_USD` ($2), `PIN_SUGGEST_PER_VISITOR` (300), `PIN_SUGGEST_CALLS_PER_DAY` (4000). The page asks 450 ms after a new note stops moving; a 429 or 503 stops asking for the visit.
- `POST /api/pin/learn` (JSON `{ notes: [{ id, works, doesNotWork, label?, topics, source? }], rules: [{ id, text, context, topics, status, basis?, question? }], shown }`, up to 30 new notes and 200 rules, same-origin only, D87): reads the new notes against the library's rules, from words only (no image, no file name), on `PIN_LEARN_MODEL` (default `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium, with the server-side refusal fallback). Returns `{ strengthen: [{ rule, notes }], propose: [{ rule, context, topics, notes, conflictsWith?, conflict?, basis, reading?, question?: { ask, options: [{ answer, rule }] }, principles?, locked? }], preview }`: the rules each note supports, and the new rules the notes teach, overlapping notes merged into one, each with where it holds, and a conflict named when a new rule contradicts one in the library. It reads with the expert foundation (D90): each rule says whether the notes state it or it is Pinch's reading of vaguer words (`reading`), names the principles that helped (`principles`, ids only), and where the words could mean designs that differ it asks a question with two to four answers instead (the rule waits as its first answer, out of exports and the agent connection, until the author answers). The page sends short ids (`n1`, `r1`) and maps them back. Anyone may call it: a visitor who hasn't bought gets a preview, `PREVIEW_RULES` (2) rules in full counting those already shown, the rest as `locked` placeholders with their topics and notes but never their words, readings or questions; the rules shown are the surest (none waiting on a question, then those drawn from the most notes). Stores nothing and logs no note text. Off with `PIN_LEARN=0`, `PIN_AI=0` or no key. Ledgers `pin-notes/learn/<day>.json` for buyers (`PIN_LEARN_DAILY_USD` $10, `PIN_LEARN_PER_VISITOR` 60, `PIN_LEARN_CALLS_PER_DAY` 600) and `pin-notes/learn-preview/<day>.json` for previews (`PIN_LEARN_PREVIEW_DAILY_USD` $3, `PIN_LEARN_PREVIEW_PER_VISITOR` 4, `PIN_LEARN_PREVIEW_CALLS_PER_DAY` 200); each call reserves its worst case ($0.40) and settles to what it cost, about $0.01 to $0.03 a reading.
- `POST /api/pin/mcp` (D89): Pinch as an MCP server on the Streamable HTTP transport, answered as JSON (a message or a batch per POST; notifications get 202; GET is 405). `Authorization: Bearer pk_…` names the account (`src/lib/agent-keys.ts`, hashes only); the account must have bought Pinch (401 without a key, 402 without the purchase). Tools (`src/lib/pin/agent.ts`): `get_design_rules` (`topics`, `tags`; a tag brings its rules with the untagged ones) returns the rules as text with where each holds and the notes it draws on; `list_rule_topics` counts them. The rules are read from the account at each call; no AI runs. `GET/POST/DELETE /api/pin/agent-key` (buyers, same-origin): whether there is a key, a new one shown once with the address, or none.
- **Accounts and paying (D81).** `GET /api/pin/auth/google` sends the browser to Google (authorization code with PKCE; the state and verifier wait in the `pinch-oauth` cookie for ten minutes); `GET /api/pin/auth/callback` checks the state, trades the code for an ID token at Google's token endpoint, checks its issuer, audience, expiry and verified email, touches `pinch-accounts/<key>.json` (the key a hash of the Google id) and sets `pinch-session`, a cookie signed with `AUTH_SECRET` (HMAC, 30 days, HttpOnly, Secure, Lax); failures land on `/?signin=failed`. `POST /api/pin/auth/signout` clears it. `POST /api/pin/auth/test` signs in without Google, only with `PINCH_TEST_LOGIN=1` and never on Vercel. `GET /api/pin/me` says whether billing is on, who is signed in, whether they may use Pinch, the personal checkout link (the Lemon Squeezy link with `checkout[email]` and `checkout[custom][user]`), and where their collections live. `POST /api/pin/billing/webhook` takes Lemon Squeezy's `order_created` and `order_refunded`, signed in `X-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body), checks the store and product, and keeps each order under `pinch-purchases/<hash of email>.json` (the buyer's email, and the account's when the checkout carried it); a later report of an order wins, so a refund holds. `POST /api/pin/billing/restore` asks Lemon Squeezy's API for a paid order by email, with `LEMONSQUEEZY_API_KEY`. `DELETE /api/pin/account` deletes the account and its folder and signs out; the purchase stays. **The gate** (`src/lib/access.ts`): with sign-in and billing both configured, suggestions and the account's rules answer 401 without a session and 402 without a paid order (cached a minute per server instance), and reading notes into rules answers with a preview; exports are the page's own, and it asks to buy before any. Otherwise nothing is gated.
- **The account's collections (D81).** `GET /api/pin/cloud/collections` lists them; `PUT` and `DELETE /api/pin/cloud/collections/<id>` write one whole or delete it with its batches. A batch's export zip goes from the browser straight to the store: `uploadPresigned` asks `POST /api/pin/cloud/upload`, which signs a put-only URL for that path in the account's own `batches/` folder (zips, 300 MB, fifteen minutes; needs `BLOB_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY`). `GET /api/pin/cloud/batches/<id>` answers with a presigned get URL, or `?raw=1` streams the bytes (local runs keep them in memory; the page falls back to it when the store's URL doesn't load); `DELETE` removes it. Keys: `pinch-users/<account>/collections/<id>.json`, `pinch-users/<account>/batches/<id>.zip`.
- `POST /api/pin/notes` (`{ entries }`, same-origin only, 403 otherwise): keeps the text of written notes. The page sends what changed in batches of 40, six seconds after typing stops and when the tab is hidden (fingerprints of what was sent are kept in `localStorage` under `pinch-sent`). Each day's document `pin-notes/text/<day>.json` holds 3,000 notes and 600 new ones per visitor (429 past that); the first write of a day deletes days older than 90. After the response, new or edited notes are tagged by area with Jev (`after()`; ledger `pin-notes/tags/<day>.json`, `PIN_TAG_CALLS_PER_DAY` default 2000; off with `PIN_TAGS=0` or no `JEV_API_KEY`); a failed call leaves the note untagged until its text changes.
- `GET /api/pin/notes` behind `ADMIN_PASSWORD` (Basic auth; 404 when unset in production, open in `next dev`): the saved days, one day with `?day=YYYY-MM-DD`, or a month's area counts with `?summary=YYYY-MM` (`?day=…&summary=1` for one day).
- Every `/api` response carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and `Cache-Control: no-store`; `next.config.ts` adds the robots header to any `/api` path as well.

## Storage

- Browser: IndexedDB `pin-notes` (stores `images`, `state`) and `pinch-collections` (stores `collections`, `batches`; collections kept without an account); `localStorage` `pinch-suggestions` (the Suggestions switch), `pinch-sent`, `pinch-orphans`, `pinch-collection` (the open collection; no longer written since D86), `pinch-save-notes` (`off` when the visitor stopped note saving, D82), `genui-theme`; cookies `pinch-session` and, during sign-in, `pinch-oauth`.
- Server: the private Blob store under `pin-notes/` (above), `pinch-accounts/`, `pinch-purchases/` and `pinch-users/` (D81). Without `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` or `BLOB_STORE_ID`, the same code runs on in-memory documents.

## Deploy

Vercel, from this repository's `main`; the live origin is https://get-pinch.io (`pinch-tool.vercel.app` serves the same deployment; sign-in and the browser's session belong to each origin, so the vercel.app ones are to redirect to it at launch). Steps and the variables are in [README.md](../README.md#deploying-to-vercel). Keeping the origin keeps every visitor's session; connecting the same Blob store keeps the saved notes and the day's ledgers.
