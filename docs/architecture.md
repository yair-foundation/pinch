# Architecture

Pinch is a Next.js 16 app (App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, TypeScript, pnpm), deployed on Vercel. One tool page and a terms page, route handlers under `/api/pin/`, no database but a private Vercel Blob store for JSON documents and, for signed-in buyers, their collections' zips. It began as the `/pin` route of the GenUI repository and was moved out whole on 2026-10-19; nothing of that site remains here.

## Repo map

- `src/app/layout.tsx`: fonts (Geist, Geist Mono through `next/font`), the page metadata (title "Pinch: tag screenshots for your agent", description, Open Graph, icon `/pin-notes/icon.svg`) and the theme provider. `src/app/page.tsx` renders the app at `/`. `src/app/robots.ts` allows `/` and disallows `/api/`. `src/app/global.css` holds the tokens (light and dark, `@custom-variant dark`), the Base UI state variants, the radius scale and the base layer.
- `src/components/site/theme-provider.tsx`: next-themes (`attribute="class"`, dark by default, system honoured, storage key `genui-theme`, kept so existing browsers keep their choice) and the `D` hotkey that toggles the theme outside fields. `theme-toggle.tsx` is the header button.
- `src/components/ui/`: `button.tsx`, `switch.tsx`, `tooltip.tsx` on Base UI, in the shadcn style (the only three Pinch uses).
- `src/components/pin/`: the app. `pin-app.tsx` (the workspace: header, landing, filmstrip, stage, notes panel, toast, shortcuts dialog, history with undo and redo, the Pin / Area / Arrow toolbar, the privacy, shortcuts and new-batch dialogs, the desktop-only screen on phones, the 100-image cap), `dialog.tsx` (the focus-trapped dialog and the shared 44px hit-area class), `collections.tsx` (the collections, shown in the batch's place from the header's Collections button: the list, a collection's rules by topic, each with its close-up and note, editable, with its topics, scope and delete; its batches; drafting; the export dialog, which picks scopes and topics and downloads the zip or copies the rules as text; collection files; moving this browser's collections into the account) and `collections-store.ts` (its own IndexedDB database `pinch-collections`: one record per collection, each batch's export zip by its id), `topic-style.ts` (the topic tags' tints and hit area), `topic-picker.tsx` (the tags a note or a rule is about), `account.tsx` (`/api/pin/me` in the page, the landing's sign-in and buy gate, the Account dialog), `cloud-store.ts` and `collections-backend.ts` (the account's collections through the cloud routes, and the interface both stores share), `drop-folder.tsx` (the landing's folder animation), `brand.tsx` (the wordmark), `store.ts` (the session in IndexedDB: database `pin-notes`, images as Blobs by id, the assets and marks as one record written on every change) and `render.ts` (canvas work: image preparation, the marked copy of each image, a crop per note, the crops sent for suggestions, the export zip and download).
- `src/lib/pin/`: pure code, tested in `__tests__/`. `model.ts` (assets, marks as dots, areas or arrows, sources, topics (`TOPICS`), suggestions, `settleCrop`, the export's README, `AGENTS.md` and `notes.json` text), `sheet.ts` (contact sheet row planning), `zip.ts` (a stored-entry zip writer), `summary.ts` (the text copied for an agent), `suggest.ts` (the prompt, its Zod output schema with the topics, and the filter that drops sentences about the overlay), `unzip.ts` (reads a zip back: stored entries, or deflated ones through `DecompressionStream`), `import.ts` (an export's `notes.json` back into assets and marks, and into a collection's notes), `learnings.ts` (collections, batches, learnings, grouping by topic, the rules file `design-rules.md` and `rules.json`), `learn.ts` (the rule-draft request, prompt and schema), `visitor.ts` (the trusted visitor address), `notes-log.ts` (what the browser sends and how a day's document takes it), `notes-store.ts` (the day documents in Blob), `notes-tags.ts` (areas, Jev questions, tags, month summary) and `notes-tagger.ts` (the calls to Jev after a save), `ai-budget.ts` (one daily ledger per AI step).
- `src/lib/`: `blob-doc.ts` (JSON documents in the private Blob store changed by compare-and-swap on the ETag, in memory without Blob credentials), `budget.ts` (prices per model, `costUsd`, `refusal`, `visitorKey` salted by `VISITOR_SALT`), `jev.ts` (`askJevQuestions`, `THRESHOLD`, `JEV_MODEL`, `JEV_ENDPOINT`), `auth.ts` (Basic auth against `ADMIN_PASSWORD`, realm "Pinch admin"; `isSameOriginWrite`), `image.ts` (`sniffImage`), `utils.ts` (`cn`). Tests in `src/lib/__tests__/`.
- `public/pin-notes/`: `icon.svg` (favicon, the wordmark's "p") and `landing.webp` (the dusk painting behind the landing card, shown in dark mode) and `landing-night.webp` (the night version, faded over it in light mode).
- `scripts/`: `jev-stub.mjs` (a local stand-in for Jev) and `format.mjs` (Prettier on named files).

## How it works

Client side: the session (images as Blobs, assets and marks as one record) lives in IndexedDB and is written on every change; images removed from the session stay until no undo snapshot holds them (`pinch-orphans` in `localStorage` names the ones waiting). The export draws a marked copy of each image, a crop per note (its edges settled on quiet bands of the image, `settleCrop`) and a contact sheet of the batch on canvas and zips them with `README.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `notes.json`. On a dot the number sits beside the point on a leader; on an area just outside its corner; an arrow runs from its numbered tail to its head. Each image may carry a source (Ours, Competitor, Other product, Concept; "made by" and a link stay in the browser). Each note may carry topics (Visual, Layout, Copy, Interaction, Flow, Accessibility), picked as chips or taken with a suggestion; they go into the export and the copied text, never to the server. A Pinch export (`.zip`) dropped or chosen opens into the session: its images and notes join what is there (`unzip.ts`, `import.ts`), which is how a batch moves to another browser. Collections (D79, D80) take the batch's place from the header: each keeps batches as their export zips and one learning per note (the rule it teaches, empty until drafted or written; personal taste or this project, by default this project for an Ours image), grouped by its first topic. Keeping a rule is agreeing with it: the author edits or deletes the ones that miss (D80). Draft sends the unwritten ones' words to `/api/pin/learn` twelve at a time; the export dialog picks scopes and topics and zips `design-rules.md`, `rules.json` and each rule's close-up, read from its batch's zip, as `evidence/r01.jpg` and on, or copies the rules as text for a chat. A collection downloads as one file (`collection.json` and its batches' zips under `batches/`) and imports with fresh ids. Signed in, the collections live in the account instead of the browser (D81), and the browser's own can be moved there. The batch's keys are off while collections show. Keys: arrows or J/K move between images, 1–9 open a note, N adds one, A toggles the Arrow tool, S cycles the source, Delete removes the open note, Escape closes it, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo, ? lists them.

## Routes

- `GET /` the tool. `GET /robots.txt`.
- `POST /api/pin/suggest` (multipart `image`, `context`, `label?`, `source?`, `shape?`): drafts what works and what doesn't, and one or two topics, for an empty note from a close-up and the whole screen (1024px and 768px JPEGs, marked in magenta), on `PIN_SUGGEST_MODEL` (default `claude-haiku-5-5`, no fallback: a refusal just means no suggestion). Stores nothing. Off with `PIN_SUGGEST=0`, `PIN_AI=0` or no key. Ledger `pin-notes/suggest/<day>.json`: `PIN_SUGGEST_DAILY_USD` ($2), `PIN_SUGGEST_PER_VISITOR` (300), `PIN_SUGGEST_CALLS_PER_DAY` (4000). The page asks 450 ms after a new note stops moving; a 429 or 503 stops asking for the visit.
- `POST /api/pin/learn` (JSON `{ notes: [{ id, works, doesNotWork, label?, topics, source? }] }`, up to 12, same-origin only): drafts the rule each note teaches, from the notes' words only (no image, no file name), on `PIN_LEARN_MODEL` (default `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium, with the server-side refusal fallback). Returns `{ rules: { [id]: rule } }`, a rule empty when the note teaches nothing. Stores nothing and logs no note text. Off with `PIN_LEARN=0`, `PIN_AI=0` or no key. Ledger `pin-notes/learn/<day>.json`: `PIN_LEARN_DAILY_USD` ($2), `PIN_LEARN_PER_VISITOR` (60), `PIN_LEARN_CALLS_PER_DAY` (600); each call reserves its worst case ($0.17) and settles to what it cost.
- **Accounts and paying (D81).** `GET /api/pin/auth/google` sends the browser to Google (authorization code with PKCE; the state and verifier wait in the `pinch-oauth` cookie for ten minutes); `GET /api/pin/auth/callback` checks the state, trades the code for an ID token at Google's token endpoint, checks its issuer, audience, expiry and verified email, touches `pinch-accounts/<key>.json` (the key a hash of the Google id) and sets `pinch-session`, a cookie signed with `AUTH_SECRET` (HMAC, 30 days, HttpOnly, Secure, Lax); failures land on `/?signin=failed`. `POST /api/pin/auth/signout` clears it. `POST /api/pin/auth/test` signs in without Google, only with `PINCH_TEST_LOGIN=1` and never on Vercel. `GET /api/pin/me` says whether billing is on, who is signed in, whether they may use Pinch, the personal checkout link (the Lemon Squeezy link with `checkout[email]` and `checkout[custom][user]`), and where their collections live. `POST /api/pin/billing/webhook` takes Lemon Squeezy's `order_created` and `order_refunded`, signed in `X-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body), checks the store and product, and keeps each order under `pinch-purchases/<hash of email>.json` (the buyer's email, and the account's when the checkout carried it); a later report of an order wins, so a refund holds. `POST /api/pin/billing/restore` asks Lemon Squeezy's API for a paid order by email, with `LEMONSQUEEZY_API_KEY`. `DELETE /api/pin/account` deletes the account and its folder and signs out; the purchase stays. **The gate** (`src/lib/access.ts`): with sign-in and billing both configured, suggestions, rule drafts and the account's collections answer 401 without a session and 402 without a paid order (cached a minute per server instance); otherwise nothing is gated.
- **The account's collections (D81).** `GET /api/pin/cloud/collections` lists them; `PUT` and `DELETE /api/pin/cloud/collections/<id>` write one whole or delete it with its batches. A batch's export zip goes from the browser straight to the store: `uploadPresigned` asks `POST /api/pin/cloud/upload`, which signs a put-only URL for that path in the account's own `batches/` folder (zips, 300 MB, fifteen minutes; needs `BLOB_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY`). `GET /api/pin/cloud/batches/<id>` answers with a presigned get URL, or `?raw=1` streams the bytes (local runs keep them in memory; the page falls back to it when the store's URL doesn't load); `DELETE` removes it. Keys: `pinch-users/<account>/collections/<id>.json`, `pinch-users/<account>/batches/<id>.zip`.
- `POST /api/pin/notes` (`{ entries }`, same-origin only, 403 otherwise): keeps the text of written notes. The page sends what changed in batches of 40, six seconds after typing stops and when the tab is hidden (fingerprints of what was sent are kept in `localStorage` under `pinch-sent`). Each day's document `pin-notes/text/<day>.json` holds 3,000 notes and 600 new ones per visitor (429 past that); the first write of a day deletes days older than 90. After the response, new or edited notes are tagged by area with Jev (`after()`; ledger `pin-notes/tags/<day>.json`, `PIN_TAG_CALLS_PER_DAY` default 2000; off with `PIN_TAGS=0` or no `JEV_API_KEY`); a failed call leaves the note untagged until its text changes.
- `GET /api/pin/notes` behind `ADMIN_PASSWORD` (Basic auth; 404 when unset in production, open in `next dev`): the saved days, one day with `?day=YYYY-MM-DD`, or a month's area counts with `?summary=YYYY-MM` (`?day=…&summary=1` for one day).
- Every `/api` response carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and `Cache-Control: no-store`; `next.config.ts` adds the robots header to any `/api` path as well.

## Storage

- Browser: IndexedDB `pin-notes` (stores `images`, `state`) and `pinch-collections` (stores `collections`, `batches`; collections kept without an account); `localStorage` `pinch-suggestions` (the Suggestions switch), `pinch-sent`, `pinch-orphans`, `pinch-collection` (the open collection), `pinch-save-notes` (`off` when the visitor stopped note saving, D82), `genui-theme`; cookies `pinch-session` and, during sign-in, `pinch-oauth`.
- Server: the private Blob store under `pin-notes/` (above), `pinch-accounts/`, `pinch-purchases/` and `pinch-users/` (D81). Without `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` or `BLOB_STORE_ID`, the same code runs on in-memory documents.

## Deploy

Vercel, from this repository's `main`; the live origin is https://pinch-tool.vercel.app. Steps and the variables are in [README.md](../README.md#deploying-to-vercel). Keeping the origin keeps every visitor's session; connecting the same Blob store keeps the saved notes and the day's ledgers.
