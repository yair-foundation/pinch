# Architecture

Pinch is a Next.js 16 app (App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, TypeScript, pnpm), deployed on Vercel. One tool page and a terms page, route handlers under `/api/pin/`, no database but a private Vercel Blob store for JSON documents and, for signed-in buyers, their collections' zips. It began as the `/pin` route of the GenUI repository and was moved out whole on 2026-10-19; nothing of that site remains here.

## How it works

Client side: the session (images as Blobs, assets and marks as one record) lives in IndexedDB and is written on every change; images removed from the session stay until no undo snapshot holds them (`pinch-orphans` in `localStorage` names the ones waiting). The export draws a marked copy of each image, a crop per note (its edges settled on quiet bands of the image, `settleCrop`) and a contact sheet of the batch on canvas and zips them with `README.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `notes.json`. On a dot the number sits beside the point on a leader; on an area just outside its corner; an arrow runs from its numbered tail to its head. An area note may also carry a spacing check between its items (`rhythm.ts`, on the same grid), drawn and exported the same way. A note may carry measured spacing (`measure.ts`, on a one-pixel luma grid sampled when the author asks; drawn on its crop and on the stage, written into the README and `notes.json` with the image's scale). Each image may carry a source (Ours, Competitor, Other product, Concept; "made by" and a link stay in the browser). Each note may carry topics (Visual, Layout, Copy, Interaction, Flow, Accessibility), picked as chips or taken with a suggestion; they go into the export, never to the server. A Pinch export (`.zip`) dropped or chosen opens into the session: its images and notes join what is there (`unzip.ts`, `import.ts`), which is how a batch moves to another browser. Your rules (D79, D86, D87, D88), one collection per person, take the images' place from the header's Your rules button, the image screen's main one. Opening it keeps the open images' new notes (by image and words, so a note is never kept twice) as an export zip, behind the scenes, for the close-ups. Every note not yet read goes to `/api/pin/learn` thirty at a time with the rules so far, on its own; each reading strengthens rules (their new notes counted as `fresh` until the evidence is opened), adds rules, or adds one flagged as a conflict with the rule it pulls against. A rule holds its notes as references (`batchId/ref`), many to many; it is guidance as soon as it is written, and the pencil edits its words, where it holds, its topics and the author's own tags (D88). Rules show by first topic and slice by one topic or tag. Before buying, the hidden rules show as placeholders; bought, they go and their notes are read again in full. Connect agent connects over MCP or copies every rule as text for one chat (`rulesText`, the same text `get_design_rules` answers); the rules zip of `design-rules.md`, `rules.json` and close-ups went with D93, and its unused code with D100. Several collections kept from before D86 are merged into the oldest when the view opens, and learnings from before D87 become rules. Bought and signed in, the rules live in the account (D81), and this browser's move in on their own. The images' keys are off while the rules show. Keys: arrows or J/K move between images, 1–9 open a note, N adds one, A toggles the Arrow tool, S cycles the source, Delete removes the open note, Escape closes it, ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z undo and redo, ? lists them.

## Routes

- `GET /` the tool. `GET /robots.txt`.
- `POST /api/pin/suggest` (multipart `image`, `context`, `label?`, `source?`, `shape?`): drafts what works and what doesn't, and one or two topics, for an empty note from a close-up and the whole screen (1024px and 768px JPEGs, marked in magenta), on `PIN_SUGGEST_MODEL` (default `claude-haiku-5-5`, no fallback: a refusal just means no suggestion). Stores nothing. Off with `PIN_SUGGEST=0`, `PIN_AI=0` or no key. Ledger `pin-notes/suggest/<day>.json`: `PIN_SUGGEST_DAILY_USD` ($2), `PIN_SUGGEST_PER_VISITOR` (300), `PIN_SUGGEST_CALLS_PER_DAY` (4000). The page asks 450 ms after a new note stops moving; a 429 or 503 stops asking for the visit.
- `POST /api/pin/learn` (JSON `{ notes: [{ id, works, doesNotWork, label?, topics, source? }], rules: [{ id, text, context, topics, status, basis?, question? }], update: { id, notes } }`, up to 30 new notes and 200 rules, same-origin only, D87): reads the new notes against the library's rules, from words only (no image, no file name), the library one rule a line and the instructions cached (D97), on `PIN_LEARN_MODEL` (default `claude-opus-5-5`, effort medium, with the server-side refusal fallback). Returns `{ strengthen: [{ rule, notes }], propose: [{ rule, context, topics, notes, conflictsWith?, conflict?, basis, reading?, question?: { ask, options: [{ answer, rule }] }, principles? }] }`: the rules each note supports, and the new rules the notes teach, overlapping notes merged into one, each with where it holds, and a conflict named when a new rule contradicts one in the library. It reads with the expert foundation (D90): each rule says whether the notes state it or it is Pinch's reading of vaguer words (`reading`), names the principles that helped (`principles`, ids only), and where the words could mean designs that differ it asks a question with two to four answers instead (the rule waits as its first answer, out of exports and the agent connection, until the author answers). A reading without a question waits too, until the author confirms it (D91). The page sends short ids (`n1`, `r1`) and maps them back. Once billing is on, a call belongs to an update, the author's click on Update rules (D98): the first call prices the whole update (20 credits and 3 a note, `src/lib/pin/credits.ts`) and holds it in the asker's ledger, 402 when the credits aren't there; each call claims its notes as it starts, so calls at once can't read more than were priced, gives them back if its reading fails, and counts them when it reads; an update an hour on takes no more calls; `POST /api/pin/credits` (`{ finish: id }`, same-origin) ends the update, giving back the credits of the notes it didn't read (an update left running takes what it read after an hour). Buyers of the original offer spend none. Each new rule comes with a short `title` (D95). `POST /api/pin/learn/titles` (`{ rules: [{ id, text }] }`, up to 40, same-origin, for anyone, D95, D98) names rules that have none on `PIN_TITLE_MODEL` (default `claude-haiku-5-5`), on the reading's ledger below, taking no credits. Stores nothing and logs no note text. Off with `PIN_LEARN=0`, `PIN_AI=0` or no key. Daily fair-use ledgers hold on top of the credits: `pin-notes/learn/<day>.json` for buyers (`PIN_LEARN_DAILY_USD` $10, `PIN_LEARN_PER_VISITOR` 60, `PIN_LEARN_CALLS_PER_DAY` 600) and, so fresh browsers can't spend what buyers need, `pin-notes/learn-preview/<day>.json` for free credits, titles included (`PIN_LEARN_PREVIEW_DAILY_USD` $3, `PIN_LEARN_PREVIEW_PER_VISITOR` 20, `PIN_LEARN_PREVIEW_CALLS_PER_DAY` 300; the names are the old preview's); each call reserves its worst case ($0.40) and settles to what it cost, about $0.01 to $0.03 a reading.
- `POST /api/pin/mcp` (D89): Pinch as an MCP server on the Streamable HTTP transport, answered as JSON (a message or a batch per POST; notifications get 202; GET is 405). `Authorization: Bearer pk_…` names the account (`src/lib/agent-keys.ts`, hashes only); the account must have bought Pinch (401 without a key, 402 without the purchase). Tools (`src/lib/pin/agent.ts`): `get_design_rules` (`topics`, `tags`; a tag brings its rules with the untagged ones; `screenTags` keeps only the rules drawn from screenshots with one of those tags, D100) returns the rules as text with where each holds and the notes it draws on; `list_rule_topics` counts them; `get_rule_evidence` (`rule`: R3, D94) returns that rule's notes, up to eight, each as text, a JSON line with the spot's box in the close-up's pixels and the screenshot's, and the crop as a JPEG image; with `whole: true` (D99) it then adds each screenshot they are on, once, as its marked copy from the export, within what the crops leave of the 3 MB. Rules keep their number among all rules in every pick. The rules are read from the account at each call; the crops and `notes.json` from the batch zips by HTTP ranges (`batchFiles` in `src/lib/cloud.ts`, `src/lib/pin/zip-range.ts`), from memory without Blob. No AI runs. `GET/POST/DELETE /api/pin/agent-key` (buyers, same-origin): whether there is a key, a new one shown once with the address, or none.
- **Accounts and paying (D81).** `GET /api/pin/auth/google` sends the browser to Google (authorization code with PKCE; the state and verifier wait in the `pinch-oauth` cookie for ten minutes); `GET /api/pin/auth/callback` checks the state, trades the code for an ID token at Google's token endpoint, checks its issuer, audience, expiry and verified email, touches `pinch-accounts/<key>.json` (the key a hash of the Google id) and sets `pinch-session`, a cookie signed with `AUTH_SECRET` (HMAC, 30 days, HttpOnly, Secure, Lax); failures land on `/?signin=failed`. `POST /api/pin/auth/signout` clears it. `POST /api/pin/auth/test` signs in without Google, only with `PINCH_TEST_LOGIN=1` and never on Vercel. `GET /api/pin/me` says whether billing is on, who is signed in, their credits (left, bought, original offer, closed), the one offer, whether suggestions are open to them, the personal checkout link (the Lemon Squeezy link with `checkout[email]` and `checkout[custom][user]`), and where their collections live. `POST /api/pin/billing/webhook` takes Lemon Squeezy's `order_created` and `order_refunded`, signed in `X-Signature` (HMAC-SHA256 of the raw body), checks the store and product (the credits pack, `LEMONSQUEEZY_CREDITS_PRODUCT_ID`, without which billing is off, and the original offer, `LEMONSQUEEZY_PRODUCT_ID`; orders kept before D98 carry no product and count as the original), and keeps each order with its product and first paid time under `pinch-purchases/<hash of email>.json` (the buyer's email, and the account's when the checkout carried it); a later report of an order wins, so a refund holds. `POST /api/pin/billing/restore` asks Lemon Squeezy's API for every paid order by email, with `LEMONSQUEEZY_API_KEY`. `DELETE /api/pin/account` deletes the account and its folder and signs out; the purchases and credits stay. **Credits** (`src/lib/credits.ts`, D98): a ledger of updates per hashed email, or per hashed id of this browser (the `pinch-browser` cookie, set by `/api/pin/me`) before sign-in, under `pinch-credits/`; the balance is worked out each time from the free 200, the paid orders of the credits pack (2,000 each, a refund taking back what is left of its pack) and the updates, free credits first, then packs oldest first. On sign-in `/api/pin/me` moves the browser's updates into the account's ledger and closes the browser's. **The gate** (`src/lib/access.ts`): with sign-in and billing both configured, reading takes credits; the account's rules, its agent key and MCP need a session (401); suggestions need a session and credits bought and left, or the original offer (402); an account keeps up to 200 MB of screenshots free and 2 GB once it has bought. Otherwise nothing is gated and nothing is counted.
- **The account's collections (D81).** `GET /api/pin/cloud/collections` lists them; `PUT` and `DELETE /api/pin/cloud/collections/<id>` write one whole or delete it with its batches. A batch's export zip goes from the browser straight to the store: `uploadPresigned` asks `POST /api/pin/cloud/upload`, which signs a put-only URL for that path in the account's own `batches/` folder (zips, 300 MB, fifteen minutes; needs `BLOB_WEBHOOK_PUBLIC_KEY`). `GET /api/pin/cloud/batches/<id>` answers with a presigned get URL, or `?raw=1` streams the bytes (local runs keep them in memory; the page falls back to it when the store's URL doesn't load); `DELETE` removes it. Keys: `pinch-users/<account>/collections/<id>.json`, `pinch-users/<account>/batches/<id>.zip`.
- `POST /api/pin/notes` (`{ entries }`, same-origin only, 403 otherwise): keeps the text of written notes. The page sends what changed in batches of 40, six seconds after typing stops and when the tab is hidden (fingerprints of what was sent are kept in `localStorage` under `pinch-sent`). Each day's document `pin-notes/text/<day>.json` holds 3,000 notes and 600 new ones per visitor (429 past that); the first write of a day deletes days older than 90. After the response, new or edited notes are tagged by area with Jev (`after()`; ledger `pin-notes/tags/<day>.json`, `PIN_TAG_CALLS_PER_DAY` default 2000; off with `PIN_TAGS=0` or no `JEV_API_KEY`); a failed call leaves the note untagged until its text changes.
- `GET /api/pin/notes` behind `ADMIN_PASSWORD` (Basic auth; 404 when unset in production, open in `next dev`): the saved days, one day with `?day=YYYY-MM-DD`, or a month's area counts with `?summary=YYYY-MM` (`?day=…&summary=1` for one day).
- Every `/api` response carries `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow` and `Cache-Control: no-store`; `next.config.ts` adds the robots header to any `/api` path as well.

## Storage

- Browser: IndexedDB `pin-notes` (stores `images`, `state`) and `pinch-collections` (stores `collections`, `batches`; collections kept without an account); `localStorage` `pinch-suggestions` (the Suggestions switch), `pinch-sent`, `pinch-orphans`, `pinch-collection` (the open collection; no longer written since D86), `pinch-save-notes` (`off` when the visitor stopped note saving, D82; read but no longer written since D93), `genui-theme`; cookies `pinch-session`, `pinch-browser` (this browser's random id for its credits before sign-in, D98) and, during sign-in, `pinch-oauth`.
- Server: the private Blob store under `pin-notes/` (above), `pinch-accounts/`, `pinch-purchases/`, `pinch-users/` (D81) and `pinch-credits/` (D98). Without `BLOB_READ_WRITE_TOKEN` or `BLOB_STORE_ID`, the same code runs on in-memory documents.

## Deploy

Vercel, from this repository's `main`; the live origin is https://get-pinch.io (`pinch-tool.vercel.app` serves the same deployment; sign-in and the browser's session belong to each origin, so the vercel.app ones are to redirect to it at launch). Steps and the variables are in [README.md](../README.md#deploying-to-vercel). Keeping the origin keeps every visitor's session; connecting the same Blob store keeps the saved notes and the day's ledgers.
